# Pilot Validation Plan

This plan treats LIGHT ONE as an unvalidated, non-medical PT consultation report SaaS MVP.

## Goals

1. Confirm that trainers can enter consultation and session data consistently.
2. Confirm that report drafts are understandable and useful for member counseling.
3. Identify privacy, safety, bias, and workflow risks before broader release.

## Non-claims

- No medical efficacy, diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, or prescription claim is made.
- QS/JATC thresholds, market-size assumptions, and performance targets remain `[확인필요]` until supported by pilot evidence and expert review.

## Minimum pilot evidence

- Trainer usability feedback
- Report comprehension feedback from members
- Missing-data and correction logs
- Safety-boundary violation log
- Privacy incident and consent audit log
