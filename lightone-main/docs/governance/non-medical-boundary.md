# Non-medical Boundary

LIGHT ONE is a non-medical PT consultation report SaaS concept. It supports trainers by structuring consultation notes, exercise-session records, member self-reports, and report drafts for review.

## Allowed wording

- PT consultation report
- Wellness and fitness counseling support
- Trainer-reviewed report draft
- Body-shape or posture observation reference
- Self-reported discomfort response record
- Exercise-session change summary

## Prohibited or review-required wording

The following wording must be removed from user-facing claims or marked `[확인필요]` until reviewed by legal, regulatory, and domain experts:

- Medical diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, prescription, disease-risk prediction, clinical decision support
- Claims that LIGHT ONE detects, cures, prevents, or treats a disease or injury
- Claims that QS, JATC, model accuracy, market size, revenue, retention lift, or operational performance are validated when only assumptions, synthetic data, or planning documents exist

## Required disclaimer

> LIGHT ONE does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment decisions, rehabilitation prescriptions, or disease-risk prediction. Outputs are non-medical consultation references that require trainer review.
